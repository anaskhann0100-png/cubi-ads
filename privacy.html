Privacy Policy for Cubi 3D

Last updated: October 2026

Cubi 3D ("the App") is developed as a free-to-play speedcubing and 3D cube simulation utility. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when using our service.

1. Information Collection and Use

Cubi 3D does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (such as your name, home address, or phone number) to our private servers. Game state, solve times, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App integrates third-party SDKs provided by Google to facilitate crash reporting, performance monitoring, and advertising:

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, rewarded, and app open advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising IDs, and interaction data to serve personalized or contextual advertisements.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: Collects anonymized crash logs, stack traces, and device performance metrics to help us fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Manages feature configurations and dynamic settings remotely.

3. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing device information and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at labnexon@gmail.com.
